Transparent method

Every assumption is exposed.

01

Flat ΛCDM geometry

The luminosity distance is evaluated numerically with ΩΛ = 1 − Ωm and no radiation term.

dL = (1 + z)c/H0 ∫ dz′/E(z′)
02

Effective uncertainty

The browser combines the released diagonal distance-modulus error with a user-selected intrinsic scatter.

σ²eff,i = σ²diag,i + σ²int
03

Profiled calibration

At every Ωm, the additive magnitude offset is solved analytically. This prevents H0 from masquerading as a shape measurement.

ΔM̂ = Σwi(μi − μmodel,i) / Σwi
04

Likelihood diagnostic

The profile uses independent Gaussian errors. The displayed interval is the grid crossing at Δχ² = 1 and is only conditional on this simplified model.

χ² = Σ[(μi − μmodel,i − ΔM̂)/σeff,i]²

What is deliberately not claimed

  • No use of the full statistical-plus-systematic covariance matrix.
  • No reproduction of the Pantheon+ selection-bias, calibration, peculiar-velocity, or host-mass pipeline.
  • No independent H0 measurement without an external absolute calibration.
  • No substitution of this 180-row visualization subset for the official 1,701-light-curve likelihood.
  • No “sigma” discovery statement derived from √Δχ².
